## Section 4: IELTS Practice Tasks (90 minutes)

### Exercise 4A: Writing Task 1 (20 minutes)

Describe the following process using vocabulary from Days 1-5:

**Topic:** "The diagram shows the process of how students apply for university admission."

Steps:
1. Research universities online
2. Complete application form
3. Submit required documents (transcripts, certificates)
4. Pay application fee
5. Attend interview (if required)
6. Receive admission decision
7. Accept offer and enroll

**Required words to use:** application, qualification, assessment, enrollment, scholarship, undergraduate, curriculum, institution

**Topic:** "Technology has changed the way people communicate, work, and learn. While it has brought many benefits, it has also created new problems. Discuss both views and give your opinion."
