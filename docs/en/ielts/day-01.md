## Section 1: Commonly Misspelled Words (20 words)

### 1. accommodation
- **Syllables:** ac-com-mo-da-tion (5 syllables)
- **Pronunciation:** /əˌkɑː.məˈdeɪ.ʃən/
- **Part of Speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** A place to live or stay; lodging
- **Common Mistake:** ❌ accomodation (missing one 'm')
- **Memory Tip:** TWO c's, TWO m's - "accommodate" has room for extra letters!
- **Example:** Finding affordable accommodation near the university is challenging.

### 2. colleague
- **Syllables:** col-league (2 syllables)
- **Pronunciation:** /ˈkɑː.liːɡ/
- **Part of Speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** A person you work with in a profession or business
- **Common Mistake:** ❌ collegue (missing 'a')
- **Memory Tip:** "college" + "ague" = colleague
- **Example:** My colleague and I collaborated on the research project.

### 3. terrace
- **Syllables:** ter-race (2 syllables)
- **Pronunciation:** /ˈter.əs/
- **Part of Speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** A flat outdoor area; a row of connected houses
- **Common Mistake:** ❌ terace (single 'r')
- **Memory Tip:** TWO r's for a RAISED area
- **Example:** The apartment has a beautiful terrace with city views.

### 4. wheelchair
- **Syllables:** wheel-chair (2 syllables)
- **Pronunciation:** /ˈwiːl.tʃer/
- **Part of Speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** A chair with wheels for people who cannot walk
- **Common Mistake:** ❌ wheel chair (two separate words)
- **Memory Tip:** It's ONE word - compound noun
- **Example:** The building has wheelchair access on all floors.

### 5. restaurant
- **Syllables:** res-tau-rant (3 syllables)
- **Pronunciation:** /ˈres.tər.ɑːnt/
- **Part of Speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** A place where meals are prepared and served to customers
- **Common Mistake:** ❌ restarant (missing 'u')
- **Memory Tip:** "rest-AU-rant" - think of gold (AU) service
- **Example:** We celebrated her birthday at an Italian restaurant.

### 6. necessary
- **Syllables:** nec-es-sa-ry (4 syllables)
- **Pronunciation:** /ˈnes.ə.ser.i/
- **Part of Speech:** adjective
- **Meaning:** Required; essential; needed
- **Common Mistake:** ❌ neccessary (double 'c' after 'ne')
- **Memory Tip:** ONE c, TWO s's - "it is neCeSSary"
- **Example:** Regular practice is necessary to improve your speaking skills.

### 7. separate
- **Syllables:** sep-a-rate (3 syllables)
- **Pronunciation:** /ˈsep.ər.ət/
- **Part of Speech:** adjective/verb
- **Meaning:** Not joined or touching; to divide or move apart
- **Common Mistake:** ❌ seperate (using 'e' instead of 'a')
- **Memory Tip:** There's "a rat" in separate
- **Example:** Please keep your notes separate from the reference materials.

### 8. environment
- **Syllables:** en-vi-ron-ment (4 syllables)
- **Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈvaɪ.rən.mənt/
- **Part of Speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** The natural world; surroundings or conditions
- **Common Mistake:** ❌ enviroment (missing 'n')
- **Memory Tip:** "iron" is in environment
- **Example:** Protecting the environment is everyone's responsibility.

### 9. government

### 11. February

### 13. particularly

### 17. occurrence

### 18. committee

- **Part of Speech:** noun
- **Meaning:** A group appointed for a specific function
- **Common Mistake:** ❌ commitee (single 't')
- **Memory Tip:** Double 'm', double 't', double 'e'

### 19. embarrass
- **Syllables:** em-bar-rass (3 syllables)

### 20. maintenance
- **Syllables:** main-te-nance (3 syllables)
