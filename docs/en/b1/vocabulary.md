# B1 Level Vocabulary List

**Total Words:** 1000 intermediate B1 words
**Level:** Intermediate (B1)
**Purpose:** Vocabulary for independent users in everyday and specialized contexts

---

## How to Use This List

Each word includes:
- **Syllable breakdown** - helps with pronunciation and spelling
- **Pronunciation** - IPA (International Phonetic Alphabet)
- **Part of Speech** - noun, verb, adjective, etc.
- **Meaning** - clear definition
- **Example** - contextual sentence usage

**Study Tips:**
- Learn 15-20 words per day
- Group words by topic for better retention
- Practice using words in your own sentences
- Review A1 and A2 vocabulary regularly
- Read intermediate texts to see words in context
