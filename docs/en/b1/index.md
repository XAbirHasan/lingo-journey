# B1 - Intermediate Level

Achieve independent use of English for everyday situations!

## 📊 Level Overview

**CEFR Level:** B1 (Intermediate)  
**Total Vocabulary:** 1000 words  
**Grammar Units:** 20 grammar topics  
**Speaking Scenarios:** 12 practical situations

## 🎯 What You'll Learn

At B1 level, you will be able to:

- Deal with most situations while traveling
- Produce simple connected text on familiar topics
- Describe experiences, events, dreams, and ambitions
- Give reasons and explanations for opinions and plans
- Understand the main points of clear standard speech

## ✅ Learning Path

1. **Week 1-3:** Education & technology vocabulary + Perfect tenses
2. **Week 4-6:** Environment vocabulary + Conditionals
3. **Week 7-9:** Business vocabulary + Passive voice
4. **Week 10-12:** Complete topics + Speaking fluency

## 💪 Study Tips

- Read short articles and books in English
- Watch English TV shows with subtitles
- Practice writing longer paragraphs
- Join English conversation groups
- Focus on fluency over perfection
- Start thinking in English
