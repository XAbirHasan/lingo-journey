# A2 - Elementary Level

Build on your A1 foundation and expand your English skills!

## 📊 Level Overview

**CEFR Level:** A2 (Elementary)  
**Total Vocabulary:** 750 words  
**Grammar Units:** 15 grammar topics  
**Speaking Dialogues:** 10 practical conversations

## 🎯 What You'll Learn

At A2 level, you will be able to:

- Communicate in simple routine tasks
- Describe your background and immediate environment
- Express needs in familiar situations
- Understand frequently used expressions
- Write short, simple notes and messages

## 📚 Study Materials

### [Vocabulary →](./vocabulary)

- Travel and transportation

### [Grammar Workbook →](./grammar-workbook)
15 grammar lessons including:

### [Speaking Practice →](./speaking-practice)
10 everyday dialogues:
- At the doctor
- Making travel plans
- Job interviews
- Talking about hobbies
- Describing experiences

## ✅ Learning Path

1. **Week 1-2:** Travel vocabulary + Past simple
2. **Week 3-4:** Shopping & health vocabulary + Future tense
3. **Week 5-6:** Work vocabulary + Comparatives
4. **Week 7-8:** Complete remaining topics + Speaking practice

## 💪 Study Tips

- Review A1 material if needed
- Practice past tense with your daily experiences
- Write short diary entries in English
- Listen to simple English podcasts or videos
- Try to have short conversations in English
