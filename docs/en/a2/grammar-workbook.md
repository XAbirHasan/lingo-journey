## Unit 1: Past Simple Tense

### Explanation
The past simple is used to talk about completed actions in the past.

**Regular verbs**: Add -ed
- walk → walked
- play → played
- watch → watched

**Irregular verbs**: Learn the past form
- go → went
- see → saw
- eat → ate

**Negative**: didn't + base verb
- I didn't go to the party.

**Questions**: Did + subject + base verb?
- Did you see the movie?

### Examples
- I **visited** my grandmother last week.
- She **didn't come** to work yesterday.
- **Did** they **finish** their homework?
- We **went** to Paris in 2019.

### Practice Exercises

1. I _____ (watch) TV last night.
2. She _____ (not/like) the food.
3. _____ you _____ (see) John yesterday?
4. They _____ (go) to the beach last summer.
5. He _____ (not/finish) his work on time.
6. Where _____ you _____ (buy) that dress?
7. We _____ (have) a great time at the party.
8. I _____ (not/understand) the question.
9. _____ she _____ (call) you last night?
10. The children _____ (play) in the garden.

### Answer Key
1. watched
2. didn't like
3. Did, see
4. went
5. didn't finish
6. did, buy
7. had
8. didn't understand
9. Did, call
10. played

## Unit 2: Past Continuous Tense

### Explanation
The past continuous describes actions in progress at a specific time in the past.

**Form**: was/were + verb-ing
- I was sleeping at 10 PM.
- They were watching TV.

**Use**:
1. Actions in progress at a specific past time
2. Background actions interrupted by another action
3. Two simultaneous past actions

### Examples
- I **was reading** when you called.
- What **were** you **doing** at 8 o'clock?
- While I **was cooking**, the phone rang.
- They **weren't listening** to the teacher.

### Practice Exercises

1. I _____ (sleep) when the alarm went off.
2. What _____ you _____ (do) at 6 PM yesterday?
3. She _____ (not/study) when I saw her.
4. We _____ (watch) TV all evening.
5. _____ they _____ (play) football when it started raining?
6. The sun _____ (shine) and birds _____ (sing).
7. I _____ (not/pay) attention in class.
8. He _____ (work) while everyone else _____ (sleep).
9. _____ you _____ (listen) to music?
10. The children _____ (not/make) noise.

### Answer Key
1. was sleeping
2. were, doing
3. wasn't studying
4. were watching
5. Were, playing
6. was shining, were singing
7. wasn't paying
8. was working, was sleeping
9. Were, listening
10. weren't making

## Unit 3: Comparatives and Superlatives

### Explanation

**Short adjectives** (1 syllable):
- Comparative: adjective + -er + than
  - tall → taller than
- Superlative: the + adjective + -est
  - tall → the tallest

**Long adjectives** (2+ syllables):
- Comparative: more + adjective + than
  - beautiful → more beautiful than
- Superlative: the most + adjective
  - beautiful → the most beautiful

**Irregular**:
- good → better → the best
- bad → worse → the worst
- far → farther/further → the farthest/furthest

### Examples
- This book is **more interesting than** that one.
- She is **the tallest** student in the class.
- My car is **faster than** yours.
- This is **the worst** movie I've ever seen.

### Practice Exercises

1. This exercise is _____ (easy) than the last one.
2. Mount Everest is _____ (high) mountain in the world.
3. Your idea is _____ (good) than mine.
4. This is _____ (expensive) restaurant in town.
5. She runs _____ (fast) than her brother.
6. That was _____ (bad) day of my life.
7. English is _____ (difficult) than I thought.
8. He is _____ (intelligent) student in the class.
9. This book is _____ (interesting) than the other one.
10. Today is _____ (hot) day of the year.

### Answer Key
1. easier
2. the highest
3. better
4. the most expensive
5. faster
6. the worst
7. more difficult
8. the most intelligent
9. more interesting
10. the hottest

## Unit 4: Future Tense (will and going to)

### Explanation

**Will** - for predictions, spontaneous decisions, promises
- It **will rain** tomorrow.
- I **'ll help** you with that.

**Going to** - for plans and intentions
- I **'m going to visit** my parents next week.
- They **'re going to buy** a new car.

### Examples
- I think she **will win** the competition.
- We **'re going to have** a party on Saturday.
- **Will** you **be** at home tomorrow?
- I **'m not going to tell** anyone.

### Practice Exercises

1. I think it _____ (rain) later.
2. We _____ (go) to the cinema tonight. (plan)
3. _____ you _____ (help) me, please?
4. Look at those clouds! It _____ (snow).
5. I _____ (not/forget) your birthday.
6. They _____ (get) married next month. (plan)
7. I'm tired. I _____ (go) to bed early.
8. _____ she _____ (come) to the party? (asking about plans)
9. The train _____ (leave) at 6 PM.
10. I promise I _____ (not/be) late.

### Answer Key
1. will rain
2. are going to go / 're going to go
3. Will, help
4. is going to snow / 's going to snow
5. won't forget / will not forget
6. are going to get / 're going to get
7. am going to go / 'm going to go
8. Is, going to come
9. is going to leave / leaves (timetable)
10. won't be / will not be

## Unit 5: Present Perfect Tense

### Explanation

**Form**: have/has + past participle

**Uses**:
1. Life experiences (ever, never)
2. Actions with present results
3. Unfinished time periods (today, this week)

**Time expressions**: already, yet, just, ever, never, for, since

### Examples
- I **have visited** London three times.
- She **has just finished** her homework.
- **Have** you **ever eaten** sushi?
- They **haven't arrived** yet.

### Practice Exercises

1. I _____ (never/be) to Japan.
2. _____ you _____ (finish) your homework yet?
3. She _____ (already/leave).
4. We _____ (live) here for five years.
5. He _____ (just/call) me.
6. _____ they _____ (ever/see) this movie?
7. I _____ (not/eat) anything today.
8. How long _____ you _____ (know) each other?
9. She _____ (work) here since 2020.
10. _____ he _____ (do) his homework yet?

### Answer Key
1. have never been
2. Have, finished
3. has already left
4. have lived
5. has just called
6. Have, ever seen
7. haven't eaten
8. have, known
9. has worked
10. Has, done

## Unit 6: Modal Verbs (should, must, have to, might)

### Explanation

**Should** - advice, recommendations
- You **should** see a doctor.

**Must** - strong obligation, certainty
- You **must** wear a seatbelt.
- She **must** be tired (certainty).

**Have to** - external obligation
- I **have to** work tomorrow.

**Might** - possibility
- It **might** rain later.

### Examples
- You **should** study harder.
- We **must** finish this today.
- Do I **have to** come?
- They **might** be late.

### Practice Exercises

1. You _____ (should) eat more vegetables.
2. I _____ (must) go now, or I'll be late.
3. _____ we _____ (have to) wear uniforms?
4. It _____ (might) be cold tomorrow.
5. You _____ (should/not) smoke here.
6. She _____ (must) be at least 40 years old.
7. _____ I _____ (have to) pay now?
8. They _____ (might/not) come to the party.
9. You _____ (must) listen to your parents.
10. We _____ (should) leave early to avoid traffic.

### Answer Key
1. should
2. must
3. Do, have to
4. might
5. shouldn't / should not
6. must
7. Do, have to
8. might not
9. must
10. should

## Unit 7: Countable and Uncountable Nouns

### Explanation

**Countable**: Can be counted (a book, two books)
- Use: a/an, some, many, a few

**Uncountable**: Cannot be counted (water, rice, information)
- Use: some, much, a little
- No plural form

**Quantifiers**:
- Countable: many, a few, several, a couple of
- Uncountable: much, a little, a bit of
- Both: some, any, a lot of, lots of, plenty of

### Examples
- I need **some** information.
- There are **many** books on the shelf.
- How **much** money do you have?
- There aren't **many** people here.

### Practice Exercises

1. There isn't _____ (much/many) milk left.
2. I have _____ (a few/a little) friends in Paris.
3. How _____ (much/many) sugar do you want?
4. She doesn't have _____ (much/many) time.
5. There are _____ (a lot of/much) cars in the street.
6. I need _____ (some/a) advice.
7. Can I have _____ (a few/a little) water?
8. There aren't _____ (much/many) people at the party.
9. We have _____ (a lot of/many) homework tonight.
10. Is there _____ (some/any) coffee left?

### Answer Key
1. much
2. a few
3. much
4. much
5. a lot of
6. some
7. a little
8. many
9. a lot of
10. any

## Unit 8: Question Words and Question Formation

### Explanation

**Question words**: Who, What, Where, When, Why, How, Which, Whose

**Yes/No questions**:
- Do/Does/Did + subject + verb
- Is/Are/Was/Were + subject

**Wh- questions**:
- Wh- word + auxiliary + subject + verb
- What **are** you **doing**?

### Examples
- **Where** do you live?
- **Who** is that person?
- **How many** languages do you speak?
- **Why** didn't you call me?

### Practice Exercises

6. _____ old are you?
7. _____ book is this?
8. _____ do you prefer, tea or coffee?
9. _____ did you buy that dress?
10. _____ money do you need?

### Answer Key
1. What
2. Where
3. When
4. Why
5. Who
6. How
7. Whose
8. Which
9. Where
10. How much

## Unit 9: Adverbs of Frequency and Manner

### Explanation

**Frequency adverbs** (how often): always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never
- **Position**: before main verb, after 'be'
- I **always** drink coffee.
- She is **never** late.

**Manner adverbs** (how): quickly, slowly, carefully, badly, well
- **Position**: usually after verb or object
- He drives **carefully**.
- She sings **beautifully**.

### Examples
- I **often** go to the gym.
- She **always** arrives early.
- He speaks **fluently**.
- They **never** eat meat.

### Practice Exercises

1. She _____ (always) is late. (Correct the position)
2. I _____ (sometimes) go to the cinema.
3. He drives _____ (dangerous).
4. They are _____ (usually) at home on Sundays.
5. She speaks English _____ (good).
6. We _____ (rarely) eat fast food.
7. He answered the question _____ (correct).
8. I _____ (never) have been to Australia.
9. She danced _____ (beautiful).
10. They _____ (often) visit their grandparents.

### Answer Key
1. She is always late.
2. sometimes
3. dangerously
4. usually
5. well
6. rarely
7. correctly
8. have never been
9. beautifully
10. often

## Unit 10: Infinitive and Gerund

### Explanation

**Infinitive** (to + verb): want, need, would like, plan, hope, decide
- I **want to learn** Spanish.

**Gerund** (-ing): enjoy, like, love, hate, finish, stop, mind
- She **enjoys reading** books.

**Both**: like, love, hate, start, begin
- I **like to dance** / I **like dancing**.

### Examples
- I **want to go** home.
- She **enjoys swimming**.
- They **decided to buy** a new car.
- We **finished eating** at 8 PM.

### Practice Exercises

1. I want _____ (go) to the beach.
2. She enjoys _____ (cook).
3. They decided _____ (move) to London.
4. Do you mind _____ (close) the window?
5. I'd like _____ (visit) Paris.
6. He finished _____ (write) the report.
7. We need _____ (buy) some milk.
8. She loves _____ (dance).
9. They hope _____ (see) you soon.
10. I hate _____ (wait).

### Answer Key
1. to go
2. cooking
3. to move
4. closing
5. to visit
6. writing
7. to buy
8. dancing / to dance
9. to see
10. waiting / to wait

## Unit 11: Conditional Sentences (First Conditional)

### Explanation

**First Conditional**: Real possibility in the future
**Form**: If + present simple, will + base verb

**Use**: For possible future situations and their results

### Examples
- **If** it **rains**, I **will stay** home.
- She **will pass** the exam **if** she **studies** hard.
- **If** you **don't hurry**, you **will miss** the bus.
- What **will** you **do if** you **lose** your job?

### Practice Exercises

1. If it _____ (rain), we _____ (not/go) to the park.
2. She _____ (be) happy if she _____ (get) the job.
3. If you _____ (study) hard, you _____ (pass) the exam.
4. We _____ (miss) the train if we _____ (not/hurry).
5. If he _____ (call), I _____ (tell) you.
6. You _____ (feel) better if you _____ (rest).
7. If they _____ (not/come), we _____ (start) without them.
8. She _____ (buy) a car if she _____ (have) enough money.
9. If you _____ (heat) water to 100°C, it _____ (boil).
10. I _____ (not/go) if you _____ (not/come) with me.

### Answer Key
1. rains, won't go / will not go
2. will be, gets
3. study, will pass
4. will miss, don't hurry
5. calls, will tell
6. will feel, rest
7. don't come, will start
8. will buy, has
9. heat, boils (zero conditional also acceptable: will boil)
10. won't go / will not go, don't come

## Unit 12: Relative Pronouns (who, which, that, where)

### Explanation

**Who**: for people
- The man **who** lives next door is a doctor.

**Which/That**: for things
- The book **which/that** I bought is interesting.

**Where**: for places
- The hotel **where** we stayed was beautiful.

**Whose**: for possession
- The woman **whose** car was stolen called the police.

### Examples
- I know a girl **who** speaks five languages.
- This is the house **where** I grew up.
- The movie **that** we watched was boring.
- She's the teacher **whose** class I attend.

### Practice Exercises

1. The man _____ is wearing a hat is my uncle.
2. The book _____ you lent me was great.
3. This is the restaurant _____ we had dinner last night.
4. I have a friend _____ lives in Japan.
5. The car _____ he bought is very expensive.
6. That's the woman _____ son won the competition.
7. The city _____ I was born is very small.
8. People _____ exercise regularly are healthier.
9. The hotel _____ we stayed was near the beach.
10. This is the laptop _____ I want to buy.

### Answer Key
1. who
2. which / that
3. where
4. who
5. which / that
6. whose
7. where
8. who
9. where
10. which / that

## Unit 13: Reported Speech (Statements)

### Explanation

**Direct speech**: "I am tired," she said.
**Reported speech**: She said (that) she was tired.

**Changes**:
- Present → Past
- "I/we" → he/she/they
- here → there
- today → that day

**Reporting verbs**: said, told (+ person), explained

### Examples
- "I like pizza." → He **said** he **liked** pizza.
- "I'm working." → She **said** she **was working**.
- "I can't come." → He **told** me he **couldn't** come.
- "We will help." → They **said** they **would** help.

### Practice Exercises

1. "I am hungry." → He said _____.
2. "I work in a bank." → She said _____.
3. "I can swim." → He told me _____.
4. "I will call you." → She said _____.
5. "I don't like coffee." → He said _____.
6. "I am watching TV." → She said _____.
7. "I have finished." → He told me _____.
8. "I went to Paris." → She said _____.
9. "I can't drive." → He said _____.
10. "We are leaving." → They said _____.

### Answer Key
1. (that) he was hungry
2. (that) she worked in a bank
3. (that) he could swim
4. (that) she would call me
5. (that) he didn't like coffee
6. (that) she was watching TV
7. (that) he had finished
8. (that) she had gone / went to Paris
9. (that) he couldn't drive
10. (that) they were leaving

## Unit 14: Phrasal Verbs (Common)

### Explanation

**Phrasal verbs** = verb + particle (preposition/adverb)
The meaning often changes completely.

**Common phrasal verbs**:
- **look after**: take care of
- **give up**: stop doing something
- **turn on/off**: switch on/off
- **get up**: rise from bed
- **put on**: wear clothes

### Examples
- Can you **look after** my cat while I'm away?
- She **gave up** smoking last year.
- **Turn off** the lights when you leave.
- I **get up** at 7 every day.

### Practice Exercises

1. I need to _____ (look after) my little sister tonight.
2. Please _____ (turn on) the TV.
3. What time do you _____ (get up)?
4. He _____ (gave up) smoking.
5. _____ (put on) your coat, it's cold.
6. Can you _____ (turn off) the music?
7. She _____ (look for) her keys everywhere.
8. I'll _____ (pick up) the children from school.
9. They _____ (break up) last month.
10. We need to _____ (fill in) this form.

### Answer Key
1. look after
2. turn on
3. get up
4. gave up
5. Put on
6. turn off
7. looked for / is looking for
8. pick up
9. broke up
10. fill in

## Unit 15: Prepositions of Time and Place (Extended)

### Explanation

**Time**:
- **at**: specific times (at 5 PM, at night, at Christmas)
- **on**: days and dates (on Monday, on June 5th)
- **in**: months, years, periods (in May, in 2023, in the morning)
- **for**: duration (for two hours)
- **since**: starting point (since Monday)
- **during**: throughout a period (during the summer)

**Place**:
- **in**: enclosed spaces (in the room, in London)
- **on**: surfaces (on the table, on the wall)
- **at**: specific points (at the bus stop, at home)
- **between**: two things
- **among**: more than two
- **opposite**: across from
- **next to/beside**: near

### Examples
- The meeting is **at** 3 PM **on** Friday.
- I've lived here **since** 2020 **for** four years.
- The book is **on** the table **in** the kitchen.
- She sat **between** Tom and Mary.

### Practice Exercises

1. I was born _____ 1995 _____ July.
2. The concert starts _____ 8 PM.
3. She has worked here _____ five years.
4. We met _____ Christmas Day.
5. The bank is _____ the post office and the bakery.
6. I'll see you _____ Monday morning.
7. They've been married _____ 2010.
8. There's a picture _____ the wall.
9. He arrived _____ the evening.
10. The cat is hiding _____ the sofa.

### Answer Key
1. in, in
2. at
3. for
4. on
5. between
6. on
7. since
8. on
9. in
10. under / behind / beside (depending on context)
