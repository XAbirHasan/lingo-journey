## Scenario 1: Introducing Yourself

### Key Vocabulary
- name, age, from, live, work/study, nice to meet you

### Useful Phrases
- My name is...
- I'm from...
- I live in...
- I work as a... / I'm a student
- Nice to meet you

### Dialogue

**Person A:** Hello! My name is Sarah. What's your name?

**Person B:** Hi Sarah! I'm Tom. Nice to meet you.

**Person A:** Nice to meet you too, Tom. Where are you from?

**Person B:** I'm from Canada. I'm from Toronto. And you?

**Person A:** I'm from London, England. But I live in New York now.

**Person B:** Oh, interesting! What do you do?

**Person A:** I'm a teacher. I teach English. What about you?

**Person B:** I'm a student. I study computer science.

**Person A:** That's great! How old are you, Tom?

**Person B:** I'm 22 years old. And you?

**Person A:** I'm 28.

### Practice Questions

Answer about yourself:
1. What's your name?
2. Where are you from?
3. Where do you live?
4. What do you do? (work/study)
5. How old are you?

## Scenario 2: Meeting Someone New

### Key Vocabulary
- Hello, Hi, How are you?, fine, good, thanks, pleased to meet you

### Useful Phrases
- How are you?
- I'm fine, thank you
- And you?
- Pleased to meet you
- Have a nice day!

### Dialogue

**John:** Hello! How are you?

**Emma:** Hi! I'm fine, thank you. And you?

**John:** I'm good, thanks. My name is John.

**Emma:** Nice to meet you, John. I'm Emma.

**John:** Pleased to meet you, Emma. Are you a student here?

**Emma:** Yes, I am. I'm in English class. What about you?

**John:** I'm a student too. I study in the morning.

**Emma:** That's nice. Do you like it here?

**John:** Yes, I do. The teachers are very good.

**Emma:** I agree! Well, I have to go now. Nice talking to you!

**John:** You too! Have a nice day!

**Emma:** Thanks! You too. Bye!

**John:** Goodbye!

### Common Greetings

**Formal:**
- Good morning (before 12 PM)
- Good afternoon (12 PM - 6 PM)
- Good evening (after 6 PM)
- Goodbye / Good night (leaving)

**Informal:**
- Hi / Hello
- Hey (very casual)
- Bye / See you / See you later

**A:** Hello! How _____ you?

**B:** I'm _____, thank you. _____ you?

**A:** I'm good. My _____ is Maria.

**B:** _____ to meet you, Maria.

## Scenario 3: At a Restaurant/Café

### Key Vocabulary
- menu, order, would like, please, thank you, bill/check

### Useful Phrases
- Can I see the menu, please?
- I would like... / I'll have...
- Can I have...?
- Anything to drink?
- The bill, please

### Dialogue

**Waiter:** Good evening! Welcome to Mario's Restaurant. Can I help you?

**Customer:** Good evening. Can I see the menu, please?

**Waiter:** Of course. Here you are.

**Customer:** Thank you. (looks at menu)

**Waiter:** Are you ready to order?

**Customer:** Yes, please. I would like a pizza.

**Waiter:** Which pizza would you like?

**Customer:** The Margherita pizza, please.

**Waiter:** Good choice! Anything to drink?

**Customer:** Yes, can I have a glass of water, please?

**Waiter:** Certainly. Still or sparkling water?

**Customer:** Still water, please.

**Waiter:** Perfect. One Margherita pizza and one still water.

**Customer:** Yes, that's right. Thank you.

(After the meal)

**Customer:** Excuse me! Can I have the bill, please?

**Waiter:** Of course. Here you are.

**Customer:** How much is it?

**Waiter:** That's $15, please.

**Customer:** Here you are. Thank you!

**Waiter:** Thank you! Have a nice evening!

### Menu Vocabulary

**Food:**
- pizza, pasta, salad, sandwich, soup, chicken, fish

**Drinks:**
- water, coffee, tea, juice, soda, wine, beer

**Desserts:**
- cake, ice cream, fruit

## Scenario 4: Shopping for Clothes

### Key Vocabulary
- size, color, try on, fitting room, expensive, cheap, buy

### Useful Phrases
- Can I help you?
- I'm looking for...
- What size are you?
- Can I try this on?
- Where is the fitting room?
- How much is it?
- I'll take it

### Dialogue

**Shop Assistant:** Hello! Can I help you?

**Customer:** Yes, please. I'm looking for a T-shirt.

**Shop Assistant:** What color would you like?

**Customer:** Do you have blue?

**Shop Assistant:** Yes, we do. What size are you?

**Customer:** I'm a medium.

**Shop Assistant:** Here you are. This is a blue T-shirt in medium.

**Customer:** Perfect! Can I try it on?

**Shop Assistant:** Of course. The fitting room is over there.

**Customer:** Thank you. (tries on the T-shirt) This is nice! How much is it?

**Shop Assistant:** It's $25.

**Customer:** OK, I'll take it.

**Shop Assistant:** Great! How would you like to pay? Cash or card?

**Customer:** Card, please.

**Shop Assistant:** Perfect. Here you are. Thank you!

**Customer:** Thank you! Goodbye!

**Shop Assistant:** Goodbye! Have a nice day!

### Sizes

- Small (S)
- Medium (M)
- Large (L)
- Extra Large (XL)

### Shopping Phrases

- It's too big / too small
- Do you have a smaller/bigger size?
- That's expensive!
- That's cheap!
- I don't like it
- I like it
- I'll think about it

### Practice Exercise

**Assistant:** Can I help you?

**You:** Yes, I'm looking for _____.

**Assistant:** What size?

**You:** _____, please.

**Assistant:** Here you are.

**You:** Can I _____ them on?

## Scenario 5: Asking for Directions

### Key Vocabulary
- where, left, right, straight, near, far, turn, street, corner

### Useful Phrases
- Excuse me, where is...?
- How do I get to...?
- Go straight
- Turn left/right
- It's on the left/right
- It's next to...
- It's near...

### Dialogue

**Tourist:** Excuse me! Can you help me?

**Local:** Of course! What do you need?

**Tourist:** Where is the train station?

**Local:** The train station? It's not far from here.

**Tourist:** How do I get there?

**Local:** Go straight on this street.

**Tourist:** OK, straight...

**Local:** Yes. Then turn left at the traffic lights.

**Tourist:** Turn left at the traffic lights. Got it.

**Local:** Then walk for about 5 minutes. The station is on your right.

**Tourist:** On my right?

**Local:** Yes, it's next to a big supermarket.

**Tourist:** OK. Straight, then left at the lights, then it's on the right.

**Local:** Exactly! You can't miss it.

**Tourist:** Thank you so much!

**Local:** You're welcome! Have a nice day!

**Tourist:** You too!

### Common Places

- bank, post office, hospital, pharmacy, supermarket, hotel, restaurant, café, museum, park, library

### Direction Words

- straight ahead
- turn left/right
- on the left/right
- at the corner
- cross the street
- opposite
- between

## Scenario 6: At the Doctor

### Key Vocabulary
- hurt, pain, sick, ill, headache, fever, medicine, appointment

### Useful Phrases
- I don't feel well
- I have a headache/fever
- It hurts
- My... hurts
- How long...?
- Take this medicine

### Dialogue

**Receptionist:** Good morning. Do you have an appointment?

**Patient:** No, I don't. But I don't feel well.

**Receptionist:** I see. Please take a seat. The doctor will see you soon.

(In the doctor's office)

**Doctor:** Hello. What's the problem?

**Patient:** I don't feel well. I have a headache and a fever.

**Doctor:** I see. How long do you have the fever?

**Patient:** Since yesterday.

**Doctor:** OK. Let me check. (checks) You have a cold.

**Patient:** Is it serious?

**Doctor:** No, it's not serious. You need to rest.

**Patient:** Should I take medicine?

**Doctor:** Yes. Take this medicine three times a day.

**Patient:** Three times a day. OK.

**Doctor:** Drink lots of water and stay in bed.

**Patient:** Thank you, doctor.

**Doctor:** You're welcome. Feel better soon!

### Body Problems

- I have a headache
- I have a stomachache
- I have a toothache
- I have a fever
- I have a cough
- I have a cold
- My back hurts
- My throat hurts

### Practice Exercise

## Scenario 7: Making Plans with Friends

### Key Vocabulary
- free, busy, maybe, sure, sounds good, what time, where, when

### Useful Phrases
- Are you free...?
- Do you want to...?
- What time?
- Sounds good!
- See you then!
- Let's meet at...

### Dialogue

**Alex:** Hi Maria! How are you?

**Maria:** Hi Alex! I'm good, thanks. And you?

**Alex:** I'm fine. Are you free on Saturday?

**Maria:** Saturday? Yes, I think so. Why?

**Alex:** Do you want to go to the cinema?

**Maria:** Oh, that sounds good! What time?

**Alex:** How about 7 PM?

**Maria:** 7 PM is perfect. What movie?

**Alex:** There's a new action movie. Do you like action movies?

**Maria:** Yes, I do! Where shall we meet?

**Alex:** Let's meet at the cinema.

**Maria:** OK. Which cinema?

**Alex:** The one on Main Street.

**Maria:** Alright! See you Saturday at 7!

**Alex:** Great! See you then!

**Maria:** Bye!

**Alex:** Bye!

### Making Suggestions

- Let's...
- How about...?
- Why don't we...?
- Do you want to...?
- Shall we...?

### Responding

**Accepting:**
- Sure!
- That sounds good!
- Great idea!
- OK!
- I'd love to!

**Declining:**
- I'm sorry, I can't
- I'm busy
- Maybe another time
- I don't think so

## Scenario 8: Talking About Daily Routine

### Key Vocabulary
- wake up, get up, have breakfast, go to work/school, finish, go home, go to bed

### Useful Phrases
- I usually...
- Every day...
- In the morning/afternoon/evening
- Then...
- After that...

### Dialogue

**Lisa:** What time do you usually wake up?

**Mark:** I usually wake up at 6:30 AM. What about you?

**Lisa:** I wake up at 7:00. I don't like waking up early!

**Mark:** Me neither! What do you do after you wake up?

**Lisa:** I have a shower, then I have breakfast.

**Mark:** What do you have for breakfast?

**Lisa:** Usually coffee and toast. And you?

**Mark:** I have cereal and juice.

**Lisa:** What time do you go to work?

**Mark:** I leave home at 8:00 and I arrive at work at 8:30.

**Lisa:** That's early! I start work at 9:00.

**Mark:** When do you finish work?

**Lisa:** I finish at 5:00 PM. Then I go home.

**Mark:** What do you do in the evening?

**Lisa:** I usually watch TV or read a book. Sometimes I meet friends.

**Mark:** Nice! What time do you go to bed?

**Lisa:** Around 11:00 PM. What about you?

**Mark:** I go to bed at 10:30. I need 8 hours of sleep!

### Time Expressions

- in the morning (6 AM - 12 PM)
- at noon/midday (12 PM)
- in the afternoon (12 PM - 6 PM)
- in the evening (6 PM - 9 PM)
- at night (9 PM - 6 AM)
- at midnight (12 AM)

- shopping list, aisle, basket, cart, checkout, bag

### Useful Phrases
- Where can I find...?
- Do you have...?
- How much is...?

- That's all

### Dialogue

**Customer:** Excuse me, where can I find the bread?

**Employee:** The bread is in aisle 3.

**Customer:** Aisle 3. Thank you!

**Employee:** You're welcome!

(At the checkout)

**Cashier:** Hello! Do you have a loyalty card?

**Customer:** No, I don't.

**Cashier:** That's OK. (scans items)

**Customer:** How much is the milk?

**Cashier:** The milk is $3.50.

**Customer:** OK, thanks.

**Cashier:** Would you like a bag?

**Customer:** Yes, please.

**Customer:** Can I pay by card?

**Cashier:** Of course.

**Customer:** Here you are.

**Cashier:** Thank you. Here's your receipt.

**Customer:** Thank you! Goodbye!

## Scenario 10: Talking About Family

### Key Vocabulary
- mother, father, sister, brother, parents, children, married, single

### Useful Phrases
- I have...
- My mother/father is...
- I'm married / single
- How many...?
- Tell me about your family

### Dialogue

**Anna:** Do you have any brothers or sisters?

**Ben:** Yes, I have one brother and one sister. What about you?

**Anna:** I have one sister. She's older than me.

**Ben:** My brother is older too. My sister is younger.

**Anna:** How old is your sister?

**Ben:** She's 18. She's a student.

**Anna:** Nice! What does your brother do?

**Ben:** He's a doctor. He works in a hospital.

**Anna:** That's interesting! What about your parents?

**Ben:** My father is a teacher and my mother is a nurse.

**Anna:** Do they live near you?

**Ben:** Yes, they live in the same city. Do you see your family often?

**Anna:** Yes, I see them every weekend. We usually have lunch together.

**Ben:** That's nice! Family is important.

**Anna:** I agree! Are you married?

**Ben:** No, I'm single. What about you?

**Anna:** I'm married. I have one daughter.

**Ben:** How old is she?

**Anna:** She's 5 years old.

**Ben:** That's a nice age!

### Family Members

- father / dad

- brother

**Extended Family:**

## Pronunciation Tips

### Important Sounds

**TH Sound:**
- Practice: this, that, think, thank
- Put tongue between teeth

**R Sound:**
- Practice: right, restaurant, three
- Don't roll the R

**V vs W:**
- Practice: very, we, water, vegetable

### Linking Words

In natural speech, we connect words:
- "Can I" sounds like "Can-nai"
- "What about" sounds like "Wha-ta-bout"
- "How are you" sounds like "How-a-ya"

### Stress and Intonation

**Questions go UP at the end:**
- Where are you FROM? ⬆
- Do you like COFFEE? ⬆

**Statements go DOWN:**
- My name is Sarah. ⬇
- I live in London. ⬇

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ I have 25 years → ✅ I am 25 years old
❌ I am agree → ✅ I agree
❌ I am boring → ✅ I am bored (or: The class is boring)
❌ I have hunger → ✅ I am hungry
❌ Where you are from? → ✅ Where are you from?
❌ I live in London since 2020 → ✅ I have lived in London since 2020 (for A2+ level)
