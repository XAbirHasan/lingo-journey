## Lesson 1: The Verb "TO BE" (Present Simple)

### Grammar Rule

The verb "to be" changes form depending on the subject:

| Subject | Verb Form | Contraction |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| I | am | I'm |
| You | are | You're |
| He/She/It | is | He's/She's/It's |
| We | are | We're |
| They | are | They're |

### Examples

- ✅ I **am** a student. = I'm a student.
- ✅ You **are** my friend. = You're my friend.
- ✅ He **is** a doctor. = He's a doctor.
- ✅ She **is** happy. = She's happy.
- ✅ It **is** a cat. = It's a cat.
- ✅ We **are** students. = We're students.
- ✅ They **are** teachers. = They're teachers.

## Lesson 3: Articles - A, An, The

### Grammar Rule

**A** = used before consonant sounds
**An** = used before vowel sounds (a, e, i, o, u)
**The** = used for specific things

### Examples

- ✅ **A** book (any book)
- ✅ **An** apple (any apple)
- ✅ **The** book on the table (specific book)

- ✅ I have **a** car.
- ✅ She eats **an** orange.
- ✅ **The** sun is bright.

### When to Use

- **A/An** for something mentioned the first time
- **The** for something mentioned again or something unique

Example:
- I see **a** dog. **The** dog is big.

## Lesson 4: Plural Nouns

### Grammar Rule

**Regular Plurals:**
- Add **-s**: book → books, car → cars
- Add **-es** after s, x, ch, sh: bus → buses, box → boxes
- Consonant + y → **-ies**: baby → babies, city → cities
- Vowel + y → **-s**: boy → boys, day → days

**Irregular Plurals:**
- man → men
- woman → women
- child → children
- person → people
- foot → feet
- tooth → teeth
- mouse → mice

### Examples

- ✅ one cat → two **cats**
- ✅ one box → three **boxes**
- ✅ one baby → four **babies**
- ✅ one man → five **men**

- ✅ It **does not rain** = It **doesn't rain** much.

**I/You/We/They** → Do + subject + base verb?

⚠️ **Important:** Remove the -s from the main verb after Does!

### Examples

- ✅ **Does** he **speak** English? → Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.
- ✅ **Does** she **live** in London? → Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.

### Short Answers

- Yes, I/you/we/they **do**. / No, I/you/we/they **don't**.
- Yes, he/she/it **does**. / No, he/she/it **doesn't**.

## Lesson 9: This, That, These, Those

### Grammar Rule

**Singular (one):**
- **This** = near (close to you)
- **That** = far (away from you)

**Plural (more than one):**
- **These** = near (close to you)
- **Those** = far (away from you)

### Examples

- ✅ **This** is my book. (one book, near)
- ✅ **That** is your pen. (one pen, far)
- ✅ **These** are my books. (many books, near)
- ✅ **Those** are your pens. (many pens, far)

## Lesson 13: Can / Can't (Ability)

### Grammar Rule

**Can** = ability or possibility

⚠️ **Important:** "Can" doesn't change! (No -s for he/she/it)

### Examples

- ✅ They **can't** come today.
- ✅ **Can** you help me?
- ✅ **Can** he play piano?

### Short Answers

- Yes, I/you/he/she/it/we/they **can**.
- No, I/you/he/she/it/we/they **can't**.

## Lesson 14: Present Continuous

### Grammar Rule

- **Form:** am/is/are + verb-ing

**Use:** Actions happening NOW

**Spelling -ing:**
- Add -ing: work → working

- ✅ He **is sleeping**.

I **am not** (I'm not) eating.
He **is not** (isn't) sleeping.
They **are not** (aren't) playing.

### Questions

**Am** I working?
**Is** he sleeping?
**Are** they playing?

- Size: big, small, large, tiny
- Color: red, blue, green, yellow

- Feeling: happy, sad, tired, hungry

## Lesson 18: Imperative (Commands)

### Grammar Rule

Use the **base form** of the verb (no subject)

**Positive command:** Base verb
**Negative command:** Don't + base verb

### Examples

- ✅ **Sit** down.
- ✅ **Open** the door.
- ✅ **Listen** to me.
- ✅ **Be** quiet.
- ✅ **Don't talk**.
- ✅ **Don't run**.
- ✅ **Don't be** late.

### Polite Commands

## Lesson 19: Past Simple - "To Be"

### Grammar Rule

| Subject | Past Form | Negative | Contraction |
|---------|-----------|----------|-------------|
| I | was | was not | wasn't |
| You | were | were not | weren't |
| He/She/It | was | was not | wasn't |
| We | were | were not | weren't |
| They | were | were not | weren't |

### Examples

- ✅ I **was** at home yesterday.
- ✅ You **were** late.
- ✅ He **was** a student.
- ✅ She **was** happy.
- ✅ We **were** tired.
- ✅ They **were** at the party.

- ✅ I **wasn't** at work.
- ✅ They **weren't** here.

### Questions

- ✅ **Was** I late?
- ✅ **Were** you at home?
- ✅ **Where were** you?
