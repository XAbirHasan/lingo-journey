# B2 - Upper Intermediate Level

Master fluent and spontaneous communication in English!

## 📊 Level Overview

**CEFR Level:** B2 (Upper Intermediate)  
**Total Vocabulary:** 1500 words  
**Grammar Units:** 20 advanced grammar topics  
**Speaking Scenarios:** 15 complex situations

## 🎯 What You'll Learn

At B2 level, you will be able to:

- Interact with native speakers with fluency and spontaneity
- Produce clear, detailed text on a wide range of subjects
- Explain viewpoints giving advantages and disadvantages
- Understand complex texts on concrete and abstract topics
- Follow extended speech and complex arguments

1. **Week 1-4:** Academic vocabulary + Advanced conditionals
2. **Week 5-8:** Business vocabulary + Perfect continuous
3. **Week 9-12:** Abstract vocabulary + Advanced structures
4. **Week 13-16:** Complete materials + Fluency mastery

## 💪 Study Tips

- Read newspapers, journals, and literature
- Watch documentaries and news in English
- Write essays and detailed reports
- Engage in debates and discussions
- Learn idiomatic expressions
- Prepare for professional or academic use
- Practice summarizing and paraphrasing
